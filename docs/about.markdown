Before attending the University of Central Florida, I graduated from Bartram Trail High School while dual-enrolled at Saint Johns River State College. I earned my Associate of Arts degree alongside my High School diploma with plans to study electrical engineering; however, my plans changed soon after my first year at UCF. I felt disingenuous about my current work and instead began to admire the complex science and history of human-drug relations and, subsequently, human biochemistry. With my former knowledge in math and digital systems, I started my new Chemistry Major with an eager desire to show my passion. After my major change with new biology and chemistry classes, I earned the UCF President's Honor Roll Certificate in the Fall of '23.

Currently, as an undergraduate, I am learning the academic theories in class and the hands-on skills in technical labs to become a certified Chemist. My degree is certified by the American Chemical Society and covers all five disciplines of chemistry: analytical, biological, inorganic, organic, and physical. With a Bachelor's focus on biochemistry, I aim to later earn a Ph.D. in Pharmacology studying behavioral neuropharmacology.

My ultimate motivation is to contribute to the scientific discussion on the medicinal treatments for mental health disorders–particularly the neurophysiology of novel pharmacological treatments for Major Depressive Disorder (MDD), Persistent Depressive Disorder (PDD), Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD), and Post-traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD).
